About

Teaching engineering. Improving manufacturing.

A profile built around technical education, applied research, and simulation-led manufacturing improvement.

My academic and professional interests centre on helping learners develop strong engineering foundations while applying CAD, simulation, and manufacturing methods to real-world problems. My research focuses on simulation-driven automation of gating system design to improve sand casting quality and reduce casting defects.

I have experience across technical education, engineering instruction, research publications, industry exposure, and software development. Before moving into full-time teaching, I also worked in the IT industry as a Programmer Analyst.

Research

Research interests & focus

Using digital engineering tools to improve process understanding, product quality, and manufacturing performance.

Primary research

Simulation-driven automation of gating system design

Developing a closed-loop approach combining CAD modelling, casting simulation, and automation to improve gating-system design.

Casting quality

Defect reduction in sand casting

Investigating shrinkage porosity, sand inclusion, blowholes, misrun, and cold shuts through process analysis and simulation.

Digital engineering

CAD–CAE integration
